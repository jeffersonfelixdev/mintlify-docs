## Description

The screen **Instance Settings** (`dash/instances/[id]/settings`) controls the behavior of the instance on send
and receive. It has two tabs: **Messages** and **Integration**.

Every change applies only after **Save settings**. The
panel confirms with *Settings updated successfully.*

The same options exist in the API. See
[Get settings](/api-reference/configurações/obter-configurações) and
[Update settings](/api-reference/configurações/atualizar-configurações).

## Messages tab

### Delay between messages

The interval the instance waits between one send and the next. The field has four
levels:

| Level | Interval |
|---|---|
| **Shortest** | 0.5 to 2 seconds |
| **Short** | 2 to 3 seconds |
| **Medium** | 5 to 7 seconds |
| **Long** | 10 to 12 seconds |

At the shortest level, the queue moves faster. At the longer levels, the send
looks more like the rhythm of a person, and a large queue takes longer to drain.
See [Message queue](/en/definitions/queue).

### Send presence update

Sends the recipient the status *typing...* or *recording audio...* before the
message.

- **On**: the recipient sees the status before the message arrives.
- **Off**: the message appears without the status.

<Note>
This feature adds a delay of 5 to 15 seconds to each message. The delay adds to
the delay between messages.
</Note>

### Enable link preview

Shows a preview of the first link of the message, based on the link metadata.

- **On**: the first link arrives with a title and a preview image.
- **Off**: the link goes as text.

It applies only to the first link of each message.

### Mark messages as read

Marks received messages as read automatically.

- **On**: the sender sees the blue double check mark as soon as the instance receives the message.
- **Off**: the message stays unread for the sender, even after your system processes it.

## Integration tab

These options apply to the behavior with AI agents. See
[Integrations](/en/panel/integrations).

### Debounce

The wait time, in seconds, before the instance sends the user messages to the AI
agent.

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| **Debounce** | Yes | Greater than 0 and less than 20 seconds |

Outside the range, the panel responds *Debounce must be greater than 0* or *Debounce must be less than 20*.

When the contact sends three messages in a row, the debounce makes the instance
wait and send the three to the agent at once, as a single context. A low value
replies faster and increases the chance of a reply before the person finishes. A
high value groups better and takes longer to reply.

### Split agent messages

Splits the agent reply into smaller parts, at the blank lines of the text.

- **On**: a long reply arrives as several messages.
- **Off**: the reply arrives as one message.

### Process image links

Converts the image links of the agent reply into image files.

- **On**: an image link becomes an image in the chat.
- **Off**: the link arrives as text.

### Reply with audio

Replies with audio to messages received as audio.

- **On**: a contact who sends audio receives audio. A contact who sends text receives text.
- **Off**: the reply is always text.

### Default ElevenLabs voice

The voice used in audio replies when the send does not specify another voice.

<Note>
The voice list depends on the ElevenLabs key registered on the instance. Without
the key, there are no voices to choose. Register the key in
[Text to speech](/en/panel/text-to-speech).
</Note>

### Disable AI on messages from the device

Disables the AI in a conversation when the line owner sends a message from the
phone, from WhatsApp Web, or from Desktop.

- **On**: when a human agent replies from the phone or from WhatsApp Web, the
  agent stops replying in that conversation. The two do not talk to the customer
  at the same time.
- **Off**: the agent continues to reply after the human intervention.
