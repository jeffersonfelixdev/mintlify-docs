## Description

The tab exists only for official API instances (WhatsApp Cloud API). On
unofficial instances, the tab does not appear.

Since **October 1, 2026**, Meta charges for **service messages**: the free-form
replies, without a template, sent inside the 24-hour customer service window.
Each number has **1,000 free service messages per month**. The charge starts at
the 1,001st delivered message. The allowance does not accumulate from one month
to the next. The month is the calendar month in the time zone of the account at
Meta.

<Warning>
**Without a payment method, messages stop.** Without a card registered at Meta,
service messages stop being delivered when they become chargeable. Customers
continue to write, but the replies do not arrive. This screen shows the usage
before that happens.
</Warning>

<Note>
**The numbers on this screen are estimates.** Meta does not offer an allowance
counter and does not report whether a card is registered. ZapperHub estimates the
allowance from the sum of delivered service messages and infers the payment
status. Official Meta data arrives with a delay of some hours. See
[Official Meta data](#official-meta-data).
</Note>

## Payment method

The first card shows the payment status and the button **Add/manage card on Meta**. The button opens the payment
methods screen of the Meta Business Suite in a new tab. The card registration is
done at Meta.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not verified** | There was no payment verification for this account yet |
| **Payment problem detected** | Meta rejected messages because of a payment problem, or marked the number as blocked for billing. The button is highlighted, and a red banner appears at the top of the instance pages |
| **No problem detected** | No sign of a payment problem |

ZapperHub infers the status from two signals: Meta error **131042** (*Business
eligibility payment issue*) on sends and events, and the health state of the
number. ZapperHub queries the health state every 6 hours. While a problem is
detected, the query happens every 15 minutes.

The status returns to **No problem detected** when two conditions occur, in any
order. The health query returns with no billing error. A message is delivered
after the problem. After you register the card, the alert disappears at the next
delivered message.

<Note>
A number blocked for another reason, such as a rejected template, does not change
the payment status.
</Note>

## Free service messages

The bar shows **N / 1,000** in the month, how many messages remain, and, when the
allowance is exhausted, how many went beyond it (**+N charged by Meta**).

Delivered service messages count. Not counted:

- Undelivered messages.
- Messages inside a free 72-hour entry window, opened by ads or contact buttons.
- Reactions sent by ZapperHub. Reactions sent from outside can appear in the estimate until the next check with Meta.

Two notices appear at the top of the instance pages, only in the current month:

| When | Notice |
|---|---|
| From **80%** of the allowance | Yellow banner: you used 80% of the free service messages of the month |
| From **100%** of the allowance | Red banner: the next service messages will be charged by Meta |

## Messages by category

Four cards show the volume of the month in each Meta pricing category
(**Marketing**, **Utility**, **Authentication**, and **Service**) and the total.
Two extra cards appear only with volume:

- **Referral conversion**: messages from conversations opened by ads that lead to WhatsApp. They do not consume the allowance.
- **Other**: pricing categories that ZapperHub does not recognize.

Each card also shows the cost, in the currency of the account at Meta:

- The cost is summed only on days already checked with Meta.
- With volume on unchecked days, the card shows **Cost to be confirmed**.
- **Cost unavailable** appears when Meta does not report
  the cost to partner apps, which is the case for accounts connected through
  ZapperHub. The volume stays official. To see the charged amount, check
  **Billing and payments** in the Meta account.

In a month with no delivery, the cards show zero and the screen reports *No messages delivered this month*.

## Official Meta data

Every 6 hours, ZapperHub checks the usage of each instance against the official
Meta data. On closed days, ZapperHub uses the Meta numbers, even when they differ
from the real-time count. A day is treated as official only some hours after it
ends.

The screen reports **Official Meta data until** a
date and time. After that, the numbers are the real-time estimate. The check also
includes messages sent by the same number outside ZapperHub.

If the check fails, the numbers do not disappear. The screen keeps the real-time
count and the last official data. It shows *Couldn't check with Meta since* with a date and the reason. ZapperHub
retries the check automatically.

<Note>
The check uses the instance token, which needs the `whatsapp_business_management`
permission. On manually connected instances, the token can lack the permission.
When this is the reason for the failure, or the token expired, the screen shows
the **Reconnect instance** button.
</Note>

## Previous months

The **Month** selector, at the top of the screen, lists the months from
the start of collection to the current month, limited to the last 13 months. In a
previous month, the screen shows the used allowance, the volume, and the cost by
category, with no notice banners.

The time zone used in the calculations appears next to the selector. If the time
zone of the account at Meta cannot be obtained, ZapperHub uses the Brasília time
zone and reports it on the screen.

The history starts on the date the feature went live. There is no retroactive
data.

## Who sees the tab

The owner of the instance, the partner who created the instance, and the sub-user
who owns it. Other users do not see the tab.

## Through the API

The same data is in
[Meta usage and billing](/api-reference/cloud-api/consumo-e-cobrança-da-meta)
(`GET /{instanceId}/billing/usage`). The call uses the `x-api-key` of the
instance. Only `cloud_api` instances accept it.

```bash
curl "https://api.zapperhub.com/{instanceId}/billing/usage" \
  -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY"
```

The optional `month` parameter (`YYYY-MM`) selects a previous month. Without it,
the API uses the current month in the time zone of the account.

```json
{
  "instanceId": "inst_123",
  "month": "2026-10",
  "isCurrentMonth": true,
  "timezone": { "name": "America/Sao_Paulo", "fallback": false },
  "currency": "BRL",
  "freeTier": {
    "used": 640,
    "limit": 1000,
    "remaining": 360,
    "overage": 0,
    "alertLevel": "none"
  },
  "categories": [
    { "category": "marketing", "volume": 120, "cost": 7.5, "costPending": false },
    { "category": "utility", "volume": 300, "cost": 2.1, "costPending": true },
    { "category": "authentication", "volume": 40, "cost": null, "costPending": false },
    { "category": "service", "volume": 900, "cost": 0, "costPending": true }
  ],
  "totalVolume": 1360,
  "dataFreshness": {
    "officialUntil": "2026-10-14T03:00:00.000Z",
    "lastReconcileAttemptAt": "2026-10-15T12:00:00.000Z",
    "lastReconcileSuccessAt": "2026-10-15T12:00:00.000Z",
    "reconcileFailure": null
  },
  "payment": {
    "status": "ok",
    "lastCheckedAt": "2026-10-15T11:55:00.000Z",
    "problemDetectedAt": null,
    "problemSignal": null,
    "billingHubUrl": "https://business.facebook.com/latest/billing_hub/payment_methods"
  },
  "availableMonths": { "first": "2026-10", "last": "2026-10" }
}
```

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `freeTier` | Service message allowance. `alertLevel` is `none`, `warning` (from 80%), or `exceeded` (from 100%). It applies only to the current month |
| `categories` | Volume and cost by category (`marketing`, `utility`, `authentication`, `service`). The four main ones always appear, even at zero. `referral_conversion` and `other` appear only with volume. `cost` is `null` with no confirmed cost. `costPending` indicates volume on unchecked days |
| `dataFreshness` | Until when the data is official. If the last check failed, `reconcileFailure` carries `kind` (`requires_reconnect`, `transient`, or `unexpected`), `since`, and `message` |
| `payment` | Inferred payment status (`not_verified`, `problem_detected`, or `ok`), the detection source (`problemSignal`), and the Meta link to register the card |
| `availableMonths` | The first and the last month that can be queried |

### Errors

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| `x-api-key` missing or from another instance | `401` |
| Instance that is not `cloud_api` | `400` |
| `month` outside the `YYYY-MM` format | `400` validation |
| `month` outside the queryable range | `400` with `code: BILLING_MONTH_UNAVAILABLE` and the first available month in `detail` |

An instance with no data yet is not an error. The response comes with zero
volumes and the payment **not verified**.

## Limitations

- The allowance is an estimate. Meta does not expose a counter.
- The payment status is inferred. Meta does not report whether a card is registered.
- Official data arrives with a delay of some hours. Meta does not report the cost
  to partner apps. The volume is official, but the charged amount is in the Meta
  account.
- The history starts on the date the feature went live and keeps at most 13 months.
- The notices appear only in the panel. There is no email or webhook for them.
