<PanelNote />

## Description

The integrations screen (`dash/instances/[id]/integrations`) has two independent
tabs: **AI agents** (*Agentes de IA*) and **Chatwoot**. Each tab connects the
instance to a different service. Both can coexist.

## AI agents tab

Links the instance to an agent that answers the conversations automatically.

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| **Integration type** (*Tipo de Integração*) | Yes | **Chatvolt**, **GPT Maker**, or **Zaia**. Error: *Tipo de integração é obrigatório* |
| **Agent ID** (*ID do Agente*) | Yes | Error: *ID do agente é obrigatório* |
| **API key** (*Chave de API*) | Yes | Error: *Chave de API é obrigatória* |

<Note>
New integrations accept only **Chatvolt**. GPT Maker and Zaia continue to work
for already registered integrations. See [AI agents](/en/integrations/ai-agents).
</Note>

After creation, change the integration with **Update** (*Atualizar*) and remove
it with **Delete integration** (*Excluir Integração*). Results: *Integração
criada com sucesso.*, *Integração excluída com sucesso.* and, on failure,
*Ocorreu um erro ao excluir a integração. Tente novamente mais tarde.*

### Control the agent

The panel shows the section **How to control the agent** (*Como controlar o
agente*), with two commands sent in the conversation itself:

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `#on` | Turns the AI on in the conversation |
| `#off` | Turns the AI off in the conversation |

The commands apply per conversation. Turning off the AI for one contact does not
affect the others. A human agent takes over one conversation without changing the
configuration.

See also the option **Disable AI on messages from the device** (*Desativar IA em
mensagens a partir do dispositivo*) in [Instance settings](/en/panel/instance-settings).
It turns off the AI in the conversation when the line owner replies from the phone.

### Follow-up webhook

For **Chatvolt** agents, the section **Webhook for follow-up and note messages**
(*Webhook para Follow up e mensagens de nota*) shows the URL to configure at
Chatvolt. Configure the webhook of the Chatvolt agent with this URL to send the
follow-up messages and the note messages. The copy button confirms with *URL
copiada com sucesso*.

The agent behavior is in [AI agents](/en/integrations/ai-agents). The grouping of
messages sent to the agent is the **Debounce**, in
[Instance settings](/en/panel/instance-settings).

## Chatwoot tab

<Warning>
**Beta.** This feature can change without notice. Errors or unexpected behavior
can occur.
</Warning>

The tab has two states: **New Chatwoot integration** (*Nova integração
Chatwoot*), with no integration, and **Active integration** (*Integração ativa*),
after creation.

### Create

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| **Chatwoot URL** (*URL do Chatwoot*) | Yes | Valid URL, such as `https://app.chatwoot.com`. Errors: *Informe a URL*, *URL inválida* |
| **Account ID** (*ID da conta*) | Yes | Numeric. Error: *ID da conta inválido* |
| **Access token** (*Token de acesso*) | Yes | The Chatwoot API access token. Error: *Informe o token de acesso* |
| **Inbox name** (*Nome da caixa de entrada*) | Yes | The name shown in Chatwoot. Error: *Informe o nome da caixa de entrada* |
| **Show agent name in messages** (*Exibir nome do atendente nas mensagens*) | No | On or off |

With **Show agent name in messages** on, the name of the Chatwoot agent is
included in the messages sent to WhatsApp. The customer sees who is talking to
them.

Click **Create integration** (*Criar integração*). Success: *Integração Chatwoot
criada com sucesso.* Failure: *Não foi possível criar a integração Chatwoot.*

### Generated identifiers

After creation, the panel shows three identifiers, generated by ZapperHub and by
Chatwoot:

| Identifier | Content |
|---|---|
| **Integration ID** (*ID da integração*) | Identifies the integration in ZapperHub |
| **Inbox ID** (*ID da caixa de entrada*) | The inbox created in Chatwoot |
| **Webhook ID** (*ID do webhook*) | The webhook created in Chatwoot to receive the events |

Keep the identifiers. They locate the integration when you investigate a
problem.

A change of the agent name display responds *Preferência atualizada.* (preference
updated) or *Não foi possível atualizar a preferência.* (update failed). If the
screen does not load, it shows *Não foi possível carregar a integração Chatwoot.*
and the **Try again** (*Tentar novamente*) button.

### Deactivate without deletion

The integration has a switch with the labels **Integration on** (*Integração
ativada*) and **Integration off** (*Integração desativada*). The change confirms
with *Status da integração atualizado com sucesso.* or fails with *Erro ao
atualizar status da integração.*

Deactivation is not deletion. On deactivation:

- The integration stops operating. Conversations stop being mirrored.
- The registration remains, with the same identifiers, URL, token, and inbox name.
- The inbox and the webhook created in Chatwoot continue to exist.
- Activation again resumes the operation, with no reconfiguration.

Use deactivation to pause temporarily.

### Delete

<Warning>
**The deletion cannot be undone.** The deletion removes the inbox and the webhook
created in your Chatwoot. To go back, create the integration again. The new inbox
will have another identifier. To stop temporarily, deactivate instead of
deleting.
</Warning>

Click **Delete Chatwoot integration** (*Excluir integração Chatwoot*). The
dialog *Excluir integração Chatwoot?* asks for confirmation. Click **Delete**
(*Excluir*) or **Cancel** (*Cancelar*). Success: *Integração Chatwoot removida.*
Failure: *Não foi possível excluir a integração Chatwoot.*
