## Create the account

Screen **Create your account** (*Crie sua conta*), at the `sign-up` route.

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| Full name (*Nome completo*) | Yes | At least 2 characters |
| Email | Yes | Valid email |
| WhatsApp number (*Número do WhatsApp*) | Yes | Valid phone number |
| Password (*Senha*) | Yes | At least 8 characters, with one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one digit, and one special character |
| Confirm password (*Confirmar senha*) | Yes | Equal to the password |
| Discount coupon (*Cupom de desconto*) | No | Appears when you check **I have a discount coupon** (*Tenho um cupom de desconto*) |
| Terms acceptance | Yes | Check **I have read and accept the terms of use and the privacy policies** (*Li e aceito os termos de uso e as políticas de privacidade*) |

The screen requires the reCAPTCHA verification before the submit.

| Error | Cause |
|---|---|
| *As senhas não coincidem* (passwords do not match) | The confirmation differs from the password |
| *Você precisa aceitar os termos de uso e a política de privacidade* (you must accept the terms) | The acceptance was not checked |

On completion, the screen shows *Conta criada com sucesso* (account created) and
opens the email verification, with the address already filled in.

## Confirm the email

There are two ways. Use the one you receive.

**6-digit code**, at the `verify-email` route. Type the code received by email in
the six fields. A wrong code responds *Código inválido* (invalid code). On
success, the screen shows *Cadastro finalizado com sucesso!* (registration
completed) and the **Login** button.

**Confirmation link**, at the `confirm-email` route. Open the link from the
email. On success, the screen shows *Email confirmado com sucesso* (email
confirmed) and redirects to the sign-in screen. If the link was already used or
expired, the screen shows *Erro ao confirmar email* (error on email
confirmation). In that case, restart the registration or ask support for a new
link.

## Sign in

Screen **Sign in to your account**, at the `sign-in` route.

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | Yes | Valid email. Error: *Invalid email* |
| **Password** | Yes | — |

The screen has two shortcuts: **Forgot your password? Click here** starts the
recovery, and **Don't have an account? Sign up for free** opens the registration.

After you sign in, you go to the instance list. See
[Panel overview](/en/panel/overview).

## Recover the password

Route `reset-password`, opened through the link sent by email.

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| New password (*Nova senha*) | Yes | At least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, digit, and special character |
| Confirmation (*Confirmação*) | Yes | Equal to the new password. Error: *As senhas devem ser iguais* |

On completion, the screen shows *Senha redefinida com sucesso* (password reset).

<Warning>
The previous password stops working immediately. Every session that depends on it
must sign in again.
</Warning>

## Complete a registration started by a third party

Route `complete-registration`. Use this screen when a partner or integrator
created your account and only the password is missing.

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| New password (*Nova senha*) | Yes | At least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, digit, and special character |
| Confirmation (*Confirmação*) | Yes | Equal to the new password. Error: *As senhas devem ser iguais* |

On completion, the screen shows *Cadastro finalizado com sucesso* (registration
completed) and redirects to the main page.

## Registration data

Screen **Account settings**, at side menu > **Settings**, route `dash/settings`.

| Field | Required | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | Error if empty: *Enter your name* |
| **Phone** | Yes | Error if empty: *Enter your phone number* |
| **Document type** | Yes | **CPF** or **CNPJ** (Brazilian tax ids). Error if empty: *Select the document type* |
| **Document number** | Yes | Error if empty: *Enter the document number* |
| **Password (optional)** | No | If filled, at least 6 characters |

Click **Save changes**. The result is *Data updated successfully!* or *Error
updating data*.

<Note>
The password field of this screen is optional and accepts 6 characters. The rule
differs from the registration password. Leave the field empty to keep the current
password.
</Note>

## Subscription and customer portal

The **My subscription** item, in the side menu, opens the
customer portal in a new tab. The portal has the invoices, the payment method,
and the cancellation.

The `complete-payment-details/[customerId]` route is used in links sent by
email. It opens the portal of that customer. If the identifier is not valid, the
screen shows *Link inválido* (invalid link).

### Overdue subscription

With an overdue subscription, the panel blocks the creation of instances. The new
instance button is disabled, with the message *You need to renew your
subscription to create an instance*.

Existing instances stay listed. The panel does not delete them. To create
instances again, regularize the payment in the customer portal, in **My
subscription**.

## Registration through a payment link (legacy)

<Warning>
**Legacy.** The `old-sign-up` route exists only for compatibility. Do not use it
for new registrations. Use `sign-up`.
</Warning>

At the `old-sign-up` route, the account is created only after a completed
payment. The screen requires the checkout session identifier in the URL. Without
it, the screen responds *Pagamento não confirmado ou link inválido* (payment not
confirmed or invalid link).

With a valid identifier, the **Create your account** screen asks only for the
password, with the same rule as the current registration.
