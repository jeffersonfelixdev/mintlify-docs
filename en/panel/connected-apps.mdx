## Description

The screen **Connected apps** (`dash/connected-apps`) lists the apps that you
connected to the [MCP server](/en/integrations/mcp) with the account login, such
as Claude or ChatGPT. For each app, you choose what it accesses and you can
remove the access at any time.

This screen does not list the account keys (`zsk_`). The account key is in
[Global API key](/en/panel/global-api-key).

## What the list shows

Each connection is a separate row. Two connected agents are two rows, even if
they are from the same app. The row has:

| Data | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name and address** | The app and its address, as on the authorization screen |
| **Instances** | **All instances** or the names of the authorized instances |
| **Access** | **Read only** or **Read and write** |
| **Connected on** | The date when you authorized |
| **Last used on** | The date of the last call of the app. **Not used yet** means that it made no call |

With no connected app, the screen shows **No connected apps**. To connect, see
[Connect with the account login](/en/integrations/mcp#connect-with-the-account-login).

## Edit the access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit">
    On the row of the app, click **Edit**. The panel opens **Edit access for** followed by the name of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the instances">
    Choose **All instances** or **Only the selected ones**. With **Only the selected ones**, select at least one instance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the access">
    Choose **Read only** or **Read and write**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Success: **Access updated.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

The options are the same as on the authorization screen. **All instances**
includes the instances that you create later. **Read only** allows 7 tools of
the MCP and **Read and write** allows all 12. The list of the tools is in
[MCP server](/en/integrations/mcp#authorization-screen).

The change takes effect on the next call of the agent. The agent does not need to
connect again.

## Revoke the access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the revocation">
    On the row of the app, click **Revoke**. The panel opens **Revoke access for** followed by the name of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Revoke access**. Success: **Access revoked.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**The revocation takes effect at once and cannot be undone.** The app loses
access to the account on its next call. To use the account again, connect the
app again.
</Warning>

The revocation affects only that connection. It does not change the account key,
the instance keys, the other connections, or the other integrations.

If another person or another tab already revoked the access, the panel shows
**This app had already lost access. The list was refreshed.**
