## Description

Chatvolt is a customer service platform with AI agents. With it, you:

- Create AI agents trained with the data of your business.
- Answer customers based on that data.
- Use more than 50 language models, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Grok.

If you do not have an account, register at
[app.chatvolt.ai/partner/zapperapi](https://app.chatvolt.ai/partner/zapperapi).

## Integrate a Chatvolt agent

Chatvolt has its own deployment for ZapperHub. The configuration is done in your
Chatvolt account.

Follow the official documentation at
[docs.chatvolt.ai/integrations/zapper](https://docs.chatvolt.ai/integrations/zapper).

For the agent behavior in ZapperHub and the `#on` and `#off` commands, see
[AI agents](/en/integrations/ai-agents).
