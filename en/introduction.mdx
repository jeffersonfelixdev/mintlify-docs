ZapperHub is an HTTP API for WhatsApp. With it, your application sends and
receives messages, manages groups, and receives events through webhooks. The
reference for each route is in the [API Reference](/api-reference) tab.

## Intended uses

ZapperHub supports these scenarios:

- Automation of pre-sales, after-sales, and support.
- Remarketing, abandoned-cart recovery, and payment reminders.
- Chatbots, including chatbots with AI agents.
- Integration with payment gateways and sales platforms.
- Integration with automation tools, such as n8n, Make, and Zapier.
- Delivery of one-time passwords (OTP).
- Integration with survey forms and lead capture.
- Direct integration with your own application.

<Warning>
**Prohibited use.** Do not use the platform for SPAM, for offensive or
discriminatory messages, or to advertise illegal products. Misuse causes the
account to be blocked. Read the
[Terms of Use](https://zapperhub.com/terms-of-use) before you start.
</Warning>
